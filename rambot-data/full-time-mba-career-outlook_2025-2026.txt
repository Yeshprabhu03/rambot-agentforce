Gabelli School Full-time MBA: Salaries, Placement and Class Profile (2025-26 Career Outlook)
Source: Gabelli "Full-time MBA 2025 & 2026 Career Outlook", linked from the Full-time MBA Internships and Job Placements page on fordham.edu (read November 7, 2026).
Use this when students ask whether the MBA is worth it, what graduates earn, or what the class looks like.

EMPLOYMENT AND SALARIES - Class of 2025 (graduates seeking employment)
- 85% accepted job offers within three months of graduation.
- 86% of international students received job offers.
- Top job functions: finance/accounting, marketing/sales, management.
- Base salary, financial services: mean $131,000; median $130,000; low $95,000; high $175,000.
- Base salary, other industries (consulting, consumer goods, healthcare, non-profit, real estate, technology): mean $102,327; median $87,500; low $52,000; high $175,000.

CLASS PROFILE
- Class of 2025: 108 students; average age 29; average 5.5 years of work experience; average GMAT 648; 63% international, 37% domestic; 40% women.
- Class of 2026: 112 students; average age 28; average 5.3 years of work experience; average GMAT 659; 50% international, 50% domestic; 39% women.

NOTES
- These figures are for the Full-time MBA only. Fordham does not publish salary figures for the specialized MS programs on its website; the MS in Quantitative Finance reports that 85-90% of graduates find jobs within three months.
- Full-time MBA tuition for the cohort starting Fall 2026 or later is $133,949 for the two-year program (2026-27 rates); merit scholarships range from $20,000 to full tuition.
