Gabelli School Graduate Programs: Guide for International Applicants (2026-2027 cycle)
Sources: Fordham Gabelli graduate admissions requirements, deadlines, MS program and international student pages (read November 6, 2026). For visa and I-20 rules, Fordham's Office for International Services is the authority.

- International students are about 70 percent of the Gabelli School graduate business population.
- Visa deadline: international applicants outside the U.S. who need an F-1 student visa must apply by Round 3. For Fall 2027 entry to on-campus MS programs and the Full-time MBA, that is March 19, 2027 (decision by May 14, 2027). Applying earlier (Round 2, January 15, 2027) also gives priority scholarship consideration and more time for the visa process.
- Applicants already in the U.S. can use Round 4 rolling admissions.

STEM-designated programs (graduates may be eligible to apply for the 24-month STEM OPT extension, for a total of up to 3 years of OPT):
- MS in Artificial Intelligence in Business
- MS in Business Analytics
- MS in Finance (on campus)
- MS in Information Technology
- MS in Marketing Intelligence
- MS in Media Management
- MS in Quantitative Finance
- Full-time MBA
Important: the Online MS in Finance is STEM-designated, but students in online programs are NOT eligible for an F-1 visa and cannot receive STEM OPT through that program. Fordham's pages do not list the MS in Accounting or MS in Management as STEM-designated; confirm with admissions.

English language proficiency (required unless you earned a U.S. degree or studied exclusively in English in an approved English-speaking country). Recommended scores: TOEFL iBT 100, IELTS 7, PTE 68, Duolingo English Test 120. Scores must be no more than two years old at the start of the term. TOEFL school code 7666.

Credential evaluation: degrees earned outside the U.S. need an official course-by-course evaluation from WES, ECE or another NACES member (exceptions for Canada, Australia, Ireland, the UK and some partner schools).

Proof of funding for the I-20: after admission, Fordham's Office for International Services issues the I-20 and requires documents showing you can cover a year of study. As a guide, Fordham's 2026-2027 estimated cost of attendance for a full-time MS student is about $92,980 for the year ($56,026 tuition and fees plus $36,954 living costs). The exact amount you must document comes from the Office for International Services. Ask the RamBot cost estimator for a program-specific estimate.

Health insurance: students are charged Fordham's health insurance ($1,890 Fall; $3,342 Spring including Summer) unless they waive it with comparable coverage. International Students Service Fee: $50.

Scholarships: international applicants are automatically considered for merit scholarships when they submit a complete application and check the scholarship box.

Support: Graduate housing in Fordham-owned apartments, an International Student Guide Book from the graduate academic advising team, and the Office for International Services. Admissions also hosts information sessions around the world (for example QS Discover Masters events in India in December 2026).
