Graduate Gabelli School of Business Tuition and Fees - 2026-2027 Academic Year
Source: https://www.fordham.edu/student-financial-services/tuition-and-payments/graduate-tuition/gabelli-school-of-business/
These are the current rates for the 2026-2027 academic year (Fall 2026, Spring 2027, Summer 2027). They replace all earlier tuition figures, including 2023-2024, 2024-2025 and 2025-2026 rates. Rates are set by Fordham Student Financial Services and can change; students should confirm their rate with Student Financial Services.

2026-2027 Academic Year Tuition

MS Programs (rate per credit)
- MS entrants between Fall 2022 and Summer 2023: $2,277 per credit
- MS entrants from Fall 2023 and later: $2,303 per credit
- MS in Strategic Marketing Communications Online (entrants Fall 2025 and Spring 2026): $1,754 per credit
Example: a full-time MS student (Fall 2023 or later entrant) taking 12 credits in Fall and 12 credits in Spring pays $27,636 tuition per term, $55,272 tuition for the year, plus $754 in required fees, for $56,026 in direct costs billed by Fordham.

Professional MBA and Pre-MBA (rate per credit)
- Entrants between Fall 2022 and Summer 2023: $2,176 per credit
- Entrants starting Fall 2023 and later: $2,303 per credit

Executive MBA Program
- Flat rate: $145,396 for five terms (Lincoln Center and Westchester campuses)
- Billed in the 2026-2027 cost calculator as $2,000 per credit plus an additional tuition charge of $1,250 in the Fall term.

Cohort (Full-Time) MBA (total program cost, paid in 4 payments over 2 years)
- Cohort beginning Fall 2026 and later: $133,949 total program cost ($33,487 per term)
- Cohort that started prior to Fall 2026: $129,107 total program cost ($32,277 per term)

Graduate School Fees (2026-2027)
- General Fee: $128 per term
- Technology Fee: $249 per term
- Total required fees: $377 per term
- Health Insurance Fee: Fall term $1,890; Spring term (includes Summer) $3,342. Students are liable for health insurance fees unless waived.
- International Students Service Fee: $50
- Maintenance of Matriculation Fee: $51

Enrichment Fees (required, one-time, charged in the first term of study only)
These fees cover educational and developmental activities such as leadership development, global knowledge and competency, personal presentation, and networking.
- Full-time MBA: $9,133
- Part-time MBA: $5,068
- MS Strategic Marketing Communication - Online students: $3,939
- MS Management - Online students: $3,939
- MS Finance - Online students: $3,939
- MS Programs - New students: $3,939

Estimated Cost of Attendance 2026-2027 (MS program, 12 credits per term, Fall and Spring)
Fordham projects these indirect costs as averages; they are approximate and not billed by Fordham.
- Food and housing: $13,555 per term ($27,110 per year)
- Books, course materials, supplies and equipment: $64 per credit ($1,536 per year at 24 credits)
- Transportation: $1,262 per term ($2,524 per year)
- Miscellaneous: $2,892 per term ($5,784 per year)
- Total indirect costs: $36,954 per year
- Estimated total cost of attendance for an MS student who entered Fall 2023 or later: $56,026 direct plus $36,954 indirect, about $92,980 per year.
