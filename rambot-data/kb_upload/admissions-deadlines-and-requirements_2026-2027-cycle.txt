Gabelli School of Business Graduate Admissions: Deadlines and Requirements for the 2026-2027 Admissions Cycle (Spring 2027 and Fall 2027 entry)
Sources: https://www.fordham.edu/gabelli-school-of-business/academic-programs-and-admissions/graduate-programs/graduate-admissions/graduate-admissions-deadlines-/ and .../graduate-admissions-requirements/ (read November 6, 2026)
These are the current deadlines. They replace the Fall 2026 and Spring 2026 deadlines, which have passed. Today is in November 2026, so Fall 2027 Round 1 (October 9, 2026) has already passed; the next Fall 2027 deadline for on-campus MS programs and the Full-time MBA is Round 2 on January 15, 2027.

FALL 2027 ENTRY - BS/MS, MS programs (on-campus), Full-time MBA, Visiting Students
- Round 1: apply by October 9, 2026; decision by December 4, 2026. Application fee waived and priority scholarship consideration.
- Round 2: apply by January 15, 2027; decision by March 19, 2027. Priority consideration for scholarships.
- Round 3: apply by March 19, 2027; decision by May 14, 2027. Last day for international applicants who need visa sponsorship from abroad.
- Round 4: rolling admissions for domestic applicants and international applicants already in the U.S.

FALL 2027 ENTRY - MS programs (online), Pre-MBA, Professional MBA, Professional MBA/MSL in Compliance, Executive MBA
- Round 1: apply by February 19, 2027; rolling decisions. Application fee waived and priority scholarship consideration.
- Round 2: apply by May 7, 2027; rolling decisions. Merit scholarship consideration.
- Round 3: apply by July 9, 2027; rolling decisions.

SPRING 2027 ENTRY (only these programs accept Spring 2027 applicants: BS/MS, MS in Accounting, MS in Media Management, Pre-MBA, Professional MBA, Professional MBA/MSL in Compliance, Executive MBA, Visiting Students)
- MS programs: apply by October 9, 2026; decision by November 13, 2026 (this deadline has passed).
- MBA programs (Pre-MBA, Professional MBA, PMBA/MSL in Compliance, Executive MBA): rolling admissions until January 8, 2027.

APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS (all MBA and specialized master's programs)
- Online application and a $150 application fee (waived for Round 1 applicants).
- Transcripts: unofficial or in-progress transcripts are accepted to apply; an official transcript is required before enrolling. A four-year bachelor's degree (or equivalent; in some cases a three-year degree) is required.
- Current resume.
- Letters of recommendation: one professional letter is REQUIRED for MBA programs; OPTIONAL (academic or professional) for specialized master's programs.
- GMAT/GRE: optional for all programs. GRE institution code 2429. GMAT codes include MS Business Analytics FMR-X6-96, MS Finance FMR-X6-40, MS Quantitative Finance FMR-X6-15, Full-time MBA FMR-X6-55, Part-time MBA FMR-X6-22, Executive MBA FMR-X6-94.
- Essays: everyone answers "Why do you want to join the Gabelli School of Business community?" (500 words). MBA applicants also answer a quantitative-readiness essay and submit a three-minute video elevator pitch. Applicants to MSAIB, MSBA, MSF, MSMI and MSQF answer a quantitative-readiness essay (350 words). An optional 150-word essay can explain gaps.
- Interviews: required if invited; 25-30 minutes; schedule at least 10 days ahead.
- Contact: admissionsgb@fordham.edu or +1 212-636-6200. Free 25-minute one-on-one Zoom "coffee chat" admissions counseling sessions are available.
