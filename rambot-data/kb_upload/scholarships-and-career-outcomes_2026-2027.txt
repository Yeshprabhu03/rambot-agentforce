Gabelli School Graduate Scholarships and Career Outcomes (current as of November 2026)
Sources: Admissions Scholarships page, Full-time MBA Internships and Job Placements page, MS in Quantitative Finance page (fordham.edu, read November 6, 2026).

MERIT SCHOLARSHIPS (no separate application; your admission application is your scholarship application; check the scholarship box)
- Specialized master's and working-professional programs (PMBA, EMBA): merit awards from $5,000 to $30,000.
- Full-time MBA: awards from $20,000 up to full tuition.
- Dean's Academic & Professional Achievement Scholarship (all MBA programs and the online Finance, Management and Strategic Marketing Communications programs): $15,000-$25,000 for the Professional and Executive MBA; $30,000-$90,000 for the Full-time MBA, spread across terms.
- Dean's Academic Excellence & Impact Scholarship (on-site specialized master's programs): $15,000-$25,000 over two fall semesters.
- Early Leaders Scholarship (current Fordham undergraduates applying to an MS program): $15,000-$25,000.
- Timing: apply by Round 1 (fee waived plus priority) or Round 2 (priority scholarship consideration). For Fall 2027 on-campus programs Round 2 is January 15, 2027. Scholarships cannot be re-evaluated after you deposit.

CAREER OUTCOMES
- Full-time MBA: for the Class of 2025, 85% of students seeking employment accepted job offers within 3 months of graduation. Recent full-time employers include American Express, Bank of America, Citigroup, EY, JPMorganChase, McKinsey & Co, PepsiCo, RBC and SMBC Nikko. Recent summer internships (Classes of 2026 and 2027) include Adobe, Amazon, AWS, Google, IBM, Microsoft, Jefferies, JP Morgan Chase, PepsiCo and TD Cowen.
- Full-time MBA salaries, Class of 2025 (base salary, graduates seeking employment; source: Gabelli "Full-time MBA 2025 & 2026 Career Outlook"): Financial services: mean $131,000, median $130,000, range $95,000-$175,000. Other industries (consulting, consumer goods, healthcare, non-profit, real estate, technology): mean $102,327, median $87,500, range $52,000-$175,000.
- Full-time MBA international students, Class of 2025: 86% received job offers. Top job functions: finance/accounting, marketing/sales, management.
- Full-time MBA class profile: Class of 2025: 108 students, average age 29, 5.5 years of work experience, average GMAT 648, 63% international, 40% women. Class of 2026: 112 students, average age 28, 5.3 years of work experience, average GMAT 659, 50% international, 39% women.
- Fordham does not publish salary figures for the MS programs on its site; for MS outcomes RamBot should share placement rates where published and suggest asking admissions.
- MS in Quantitative Finance: 85-90% of graduates find jobs within three months of graduation; within six months the figure approaches 100%.
- Gabelli alumni network: 47,000+ alumni worldwide.
- The full 2025-26 MBA Class Profile & Career Outlook is downloadable from the Full-time MBA Internships and Job Placements page.
