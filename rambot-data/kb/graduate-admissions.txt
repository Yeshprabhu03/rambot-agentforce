# Welcome to Graduate Admissions!

## Taking That First Step

The Gabelli School of Business provides a graduate business education that is second to none. From our Lincoln Center location in the heart of New York City—the business capital of the world—to our cutting-edge curricula, unsurpassed networking opportunities, responsible business focus, and global perspective, we are the preference for those who seek to gain the competitive advantage while leveraging the knowledge they acquire and the connections they forge to make the world a better place.

### Your Top Choice for Graduate Business Education

#### A Location at the Very Center of the Business World

Studying business in New York City is a transformative experience—one that opens your eyes to new possibilities while building your connections to industry. The Gabelli School learning environment offers a breadth and depth of viewpoints that reflect the City’s incredible diversity. Access to the world’s top financial services companies, big four accounting firms, world-renowned marketing and advertising agencies, and communications conglomerates gives you the New York edge that will serve you for a lifetime!

#### A Commitment to Ensuring Your Success

From your very first inquiry about pursuing a graduate degree to your enrollment and beyond, the admissions team is here to provide the guidance you need to make informed decisions. We offer a wealth of opportunities to engage with us, as well as scholarships and other forms of assistance to make your education affordable. Our round-driven admissions cycles provide a wide range of options and incentives to meet your needs.

#### A Transformational Approach to Graduate Business Education

When you enroll at the Gabelli School of Business you become part of a learning environment that provides the opportunity to grow personally and professionally. Our programs of study and renowned faculty members challenge you to solve complex business problems, think creatively and strategically, and harness the power of new technologies—setting you apart and preparing you for the challenges and opportunities ahead.

#### A Focus on Career Outcomes and Professional Success

At the Gabelli School, our approach to graduate education connects the dots between classroom learning and career outcomes. As a student enrolled in any of our programs, you will benefit from integrated academic advising, career development services, and extracurricular and networking opportunities that take your education to a whole new level and give you the competitive advantage.

#### A Responsible and Global Business Perspective

As industry becomes increasingly global, and the world focuses on conserving precious resources, providing equal opportunity, and utilizing business as a catalyst for change, it is more critical than ever before to acquire the knowledge and skills to navigate in this new environment and leverage the insights you gain to truly make a difference. While many business schools are beginning to embrace this ideology, it has been deeply ingrained in the Gabelli School’s teaching and learning for decades and will serve you well throughout your business career. Learn more about the Responsible Business Center, the heart of much of this activity.
